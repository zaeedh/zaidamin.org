Postgraduate Research Opportunities (Ph.D. & Master's by Research)

I am actively seeking highly motivated, intellectually curious postgraduate scholars (Ph.D. candidates and Master's by Research students) who wish to solve profound problems at the intersection of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), AI Governance, Cognitive Attention Systems, and Digital Society. If you want your research to move beyond incremental benchmarking and directly impact human well-being and technological policy, our group provides an intellectually rigorous and supportive home.

Open Supervised Research Directions

Ph.D. Master's

Agentic AI Safety & The Proxy Trap

Investigating how multi-agent systems diverge from human goals when optimizing surrogate rewards, and constructing verified audit mechanisms for automated civic and enterprise decision-making.

Prerequisites: Python, LLM frameworks, Reinforcement Learning, Alignment concepts, or formal evaluation methods.
Ph.D. Master's

Attention-Based Interface Interventions & Digital Well-Being

Extending the Tune-Attention model to design cognitive micro-frictions and intentional friction systems that reduce information overload, dark pattern susceptibility, and misinformation dissemination.

Prerequisites: HCI methods, Frontend/Fullstack prototyping, Eye-tracking/Behavioral analytics, Quantitative user study design.
Ph.D. Master's

Multimodal AI Orchestration for Sustainable Urban Governance

Developing verifiable, edge-deployable multi-agent systems for municipal compliance, environmental reporting, and civic citizen-in-the-loop engagement.

Prerequisites: Data Science, Computer Vision, Geospatial analytics, Agent frameworks.
Master's

Immersive Virtual Heritage & Spatial Social Computing

Pioneering interactive virtual heritage platforms (extending the Srivijayapedia initiative) using immersive VR to reconstruct lost civilizational records and explore collective memory.

Prerequisites: Unity / Unreal Engine, WebXR, 3D modeling, Cultural informatics.

How to Submit an Inquiry for Supervision

  1. Review my recent publications on Attention-Based Design (IEEE Access) or Agentic AI Governance (Smart Cities 2026).
  2. Prepare your Academic CV, transcripts, and a 1-page Research Proposal outlining your core problem.
  3. Email your materials to zaid.amin@newinti.edu.my with the subject: [Prospective Postgraduate] - [Your Name] - [Ph.D./Master].
