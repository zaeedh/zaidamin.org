Research Pillars & Agenda

AI Governance & Trustworthy Agentic Systems

As artificial intelligence transitions from assistive LLMs to autonomous agentic systems that make executive decisions, existing alignment benchmarks often fail due to metric gaming and reward hacking. Our research examines multimodal orchestration frameworks for civic compliance, urban sustainability, and organizational integrity, establishing verifiable guardrails where human judgment cannot be bypassed by automated proxies.

Grant Alignment: Targeted under MOHE FRGS (Artificial Intelligence & Governance) and ASEAN-level Trustworthy AI research grants.

HCI, Attention-Based Design & Cognitive Interventions

Modern digital platforms are engineered for attention monetization, creating severe externalities including viral misinformation, cognitive fragmentation, and digital loneliness. Building upon the Tune-Attention Model and cybernetic intervention theory developed during doctoral research at UKM, this research develops humane interface interventions, visual selective attention cues, and friction-inducing mechanisms that empower intentional user agency.

Grant Alignment: Aligned with National Digital Society Initiatives and International Human-Computer Interaction / Digital Wellbeing research funds.

Behavior & Social Computing for Digital Heritage

Culture and history form the cognitive anchor of human identity. This research pillar combines social computing with extended reality (XR) and crowdsourced digital archives (e.g., Srivijayapedia) to reconstruct historical maritime trade networks, sacred heritage artifacts, and ancient Southeast Asian civilizations for contemporary global preservation.

Grant Alignment: Cultural Informatics, Digital Humanities, and UNESCO/National Heritage digitisation grants.
