Scholarly Vision & Grants

Research Pillars & Grant Themes

Developing human-centered, ethically aligned computational frameworks that govern autonomous agentic AI, mitigate cognitive distractions in information ecosystems, and preserve cultural heritage through immersive technology.

AI Governance & Trustworthy Agentic Systems

As artificial intelligence transitions from assistive LLMs to autonomous agentic systems that make executive decisions, existing alignment benchmarks often fail due to metric gaming and reward hacking. Our research examines multimodal orchestration frameworks for civic compliance, urban sustainability, and organizational integrity, establishing verifiable guardrails where human judgment cannot be bypassed by automated proxies.

  • The Proxy Trap in Autonomous AI Decision-Making
  • Multimodal Orchestration for Civic Compliance & Smart Cities
  • Explainable & Auditable Agentic Workflows
  • Algorithmic Accountability & Policy Standards
Grant Alignment: Targeted under MOHE FRGS (Artificial Intelligence & Governance) and ASEAN-level Trustworthy AI research grants.

HCI, Attention-Based Design & Cognitive Interventions

Modern digital platforms are engineered for attention monetization, creating severe externalities including viral misinformation, cognitive fragmentation, and digital loneliness. Building upon the Tune-Attention Model and cybernetic intervention theory developed during doctoral research at UKM, this research develops humane interface interventions, visual selective attention cues, and friction-inducing mechanisms that empower intentional user agency.

  • Tune-Attention Model & Cybernetic Feedback Loops
  • Visual Selective Attention Systems for Combating Misinformation
  • Persuasive Technology & Humane Interface Engineering
  • Digital Loneliness & Well-being in Hyper-Connected Ecosystems
Grant Alignment: Aligned with National Digital Society Initiatives and International Human-Computer Interaction / Digital Wellbeing research funds.

Behavior & Social Computing for Digital Heritage

Culture and history form the cognitive anchor of human identity. This research pillar combines social computing with extended reality (XR) and crowdsourced digital archives (e.g., Srivijayapedia) to reconstruct historical maritime trade networks, sacred heritage artifacts, and ancient Southeast Asian civilizations for contemporary global preservation.

  • Srivijayapedia: Digital Platforms for Civilizational Heritage Preservation
  • Immersive Virtual Reality (VR) for Historical Digital Communication
  • Social Identity Construction & Influencer Dynamics in Social Networks
  • Community-Driven Digital Knowledge Repositories
Grant Alignment: Cultural Informatics, Digital Humanities, and UNESCO/National Heritage digitisation grants.

Academic Mentorship & Opportunities

Postgraduate Research Opportunities (Ph.D. & Master's by Research)

I am actively seeking highly motivated, intellectually curious postgraduate scholars (Ph.D. candidates and Master's by Research students) who wish to solve profound problems at the intersection of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), AI Governance, Cognitive Attention Systems, and Digital Society. If you want your research to move beyond incremental benchmarking and directly impact human well-being and technological policy, our group provides an intellectually rigorous and supportive home.

Open Supervised Research Directions

Ph.D. Master's
Agentic AI Safety & The Proxy Trap

Investigating how multi-agent systems diverge from human goals when optimizing surrogate rewards, and constructing verified audit mechanisms for automated civic and enterprise decision-making.

Skills: Python, LLM frameworks, Reinforcement Learning, Alignment concepts, or formal evaluation methods.
Ph.D. Master's
Attention-Based Interface Interventions & Digital Well-Being

Extending the Tune-Attention model to design cognitive micro-frictions and intentional friction systems that reduce information overload, dark pattern susceptibility, and misinformation dissemination.

Skills: HCI methods, Frontend/Fullstack prototyping, Eye-tracking/Behavioral analytics, Quantitative user study design.
Ph.D. Master's
Multimodal AI Orchestration for Sustainable Urban Governance

Developing verifiable, edge-deployable multi-agent systems for municipal compliance, environmental reporting, and civic citizen-in-the-loop engagement.

Skills: Data Science, Computer Vision, Geospatial analytics, Agent frameworks.
Master's
Immersive Virtual Heritage & Spatial Social Computing

Pioneering interactive virtual heritage platforms (extending the Srivijayapedia initiative) using immersive VR to reconstruct lost civilizational records and explore collective memory.

Skills: Unity / Unreal Engine, WebXR, 3D modeling, Cultural informatics.

How to Inquire for Supervision:

  1. Verify that your background in Computer Science / IT / Data Science meets postgraduate entry requirements.
  2. Read my recent papers on Attention-Based Design (IEEE Access 2021) or Agentic Governance (Smart Cities 2026).
  3. Email your CV and a 1-page Research Proposal to zaid.amin@newinti.edu.my with the subject [Prospective Postgraduate] - [Your Name].
