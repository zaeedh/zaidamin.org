Social Inequality and the Pain of Social Comparison on Social Media

An incisive op-ed exploring how social media acts as a magnifying glass for modern inequality. Examining the global phenomenon of public officials flaunting opulent lifestyles ("flexing"), from Indonesia to Nepal, the article highlights how ostentatious displays of wealth not only spark anti-corruption investigations and public outrage, but also inflict deep psychological wounds of social comparison on citizens facing economic hardship.

Polarization in the Digital Age: A Silent Threat to Unity and Democracy

An insightful op-ed examining how digital echo chambers and algorithmic curation silently drive societal polarization. Drawing on theories of social capital and digital media dynamics, the article warns that when citizens are divided by online ideological warfare, their collective power to hold authorities accountable weakens—diverting public attention away from critical issues like corruption and socio-economic inequality.
